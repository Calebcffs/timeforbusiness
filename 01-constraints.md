# 01 Constraints

Every idea is judged against this file. Update it when your situation changes.

*Last updated: 2026-10-09*

## Hard constraints

| Item | Value | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Market | Singapore (small, about 6M people, high costs, high spending power) | Favors niche, premium or B2B. Mass-market volume is limited. |
| Startup capital | **S$5k to S$20k** (amount you can afford to lose) | Small inventory runs are OK. No leases, no hiring, no heavy paid marketing at the start. |
| Time | **Part-time, about 20 hrs/week** (raised from 10 on 2026-10-09) | Roughly doubles what one person can run. Still avoid anything needing daily presence, e.g. F&B shop, event stalls every weekend. |
| Goal | **Steady income soon** (profit in 6 to 12 months) | Favors short sales cycles and recurring revenue. Penalize long build-ups and ideas that need scale before they pay. |

## Strengths

- Vibecoding: can ship MVPs of digital products (not deeply technical).
- Sales: has done direct outreach (CRM walk-in logging for Singapore).
- Comfortable selling physical products.
- Lives in Singapore, so local presence and local networks are available.

## Preferred go-to-market

**Partnerships** (distributors, gyms, clinics, other businesses who resell or refer). Implication: B2B2C and channel-led ideas get a bonus. Ideas that depend only on brand-new consumer audience building get a penalty.

## Chairman preferences (stated 2026-10-09)

- **Club and Coach Desk** (coach/club website, booking and payments) is the Chairman's personal favourite from the first shortlist, by a small margin. Treat it as the baseline: new ideas should beat it, not just match it.
- The first shortlist felt "so-so". A second, wider ideation round was requested.

## Open questions

- Day job: any employer restrictions on running a side business? (Check contract. Can matter for a sole prop or company.)
- Is there an existing network (sports coaches, pet owners, F&B contacts) to open partnership doors?
- Risk tolerance if the first idea fails: restart with a second idea, or stop?

## What these constraints rule out (for now)

- F&B outlets, retail shops, anything with a lease.
- Ideas needing more than about 20 hrs/week of ongoing manual work.
- Ideas needing S$20k+ in inventory before the first sale.
- Deep tech or ML products.
