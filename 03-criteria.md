# 03 Criteria: kill filter and scoring

## Stage A: Kill filter (any NO = idea dies)

Answer each with YES, NO or UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN means research it before deciding.

| # | Test | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Can I start with at most S$20k, ideally under S$10k? | Capital limit |
| K2 | Can it run on about 20 hrs/week after launch? | Side-project time limit |
| K3 | Is demand structural, not a fad? (Would it exist in 5 years? Not driven by a viral trend.) | User wants non-transient demand |
| K4 | Is it legal in SG with licences I can realistically get? | Regulation can block entry |
| K5 | Is there a path to the first 10 customers without a big ad budget? (Partnership, outreach, existing channel.) | Marketing is most of the battle |
| K6 | Does gross margin plausibly exceed 30% after landed cost, fees and returns? (Services and digital: above 60%.) | Small SG volumes need margin |
| K7 | Can I get to profit within 12 months on the numbers? | "Steady income soon" |

## Stage B: Weighted scoring (survivors only)

Score each 1 to 5. Weights reflect the stated goal.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 (bad) | 5 (great) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand stability | 20% | Trend or fad | Recurring need, any economy |
| Time to first revenue | 15% | 6+ months | Under 4 weeks |
| Margin and unit economics | 15% | Under 20% | 60% or more |
| Partnership fit | 15% | Needs own audience | Clear reseller or referral partners |
| Startup cost and cash risk | 10% | Near S$20k | Under S$3k |
| Competition and differentiation | 10% | Crowded, no angle | Clear wedge or underserved niche |
| Recurring revenue / repeat purchase | 10% | One-off sale | Subscription or reorder |
| Scalability / asset value | 5% | Job in disguise | Sellable, or runs without me |

Score = sum of (weight x score). Maximum is 5.0.

## Stage C: Validation tests (cheapest first)

Before spending on inventory or a build:

1. **Talk test:** 5 conversations with potential customers or partners. Do at least 2 say "I'd pay or refer for this"?
2. **Landing page test:** simple page plus a small spend or outreach. Count signups or deposits.
3. **Preorder or pilot:** take money, or run a free pilot with a partner, before producing anything.

An idea is greenlit only if it passes at least the talk test plus one of the other two.

## Evidence standard

- Each claim in `research/<idea>.md` gets a source URL and a "checked" date.
- Mark a number as **estimate** if it is not sourced.
- Market data older than 12 months gets re-checked before the decision.
