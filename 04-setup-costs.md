# 04 Setup costs (any SG business)

*Checked 2026-10-08 via web search. Fees change. Confirm on official sites before paying.*

## Entity

| Option | Registration cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | S$115 year 1 (S$15 name + S$100 registration); renewal S$175/yr or S$300 for 3 yrs | Cheapest and simplest. No liability protection: personal assets are exposed. |
| Private limited (Pte Ltd) | S$315 (S$15 name + S$300 incorporation) | Limited liability. More compliance (annual filing, accounts, usually a corporate secretary). Better if you want partners, investors or to sell it later. |

Sources: [ACRA fees for sole proprietorships](https://www.acra.gov.sg/manage/sole-proprietorship-partnerships/service-transaction-fees), [You.co incorporation guide](https://www.you.co/biz/blog/company-incorporation-singapore/).

**Working recommendation:** sole prop to test a low-risk idea; Pte Ltd before taking on inventory liability, partner contracts or a food/pet product. Revisit once an idea is chosen.

## Tax

- GST registration is mandatory only above **S$1M** taxable turnover. Not relevant at start. GST rate is 9%.
- Corporate tax is a flat **17%**. New companies get a Start-Up Tax Exemption for the first 3 years: 75% off the first S$100k of chargeable income, 50% off the next S$100k.
- Sole prop profit is taxed as personal income.

Source: [Healy Consultants SG tax 2026](https://www.healyconsultants.com/singapore-company-registration/accounting-tax/).

## Sector-specific licensing (found so far)

### Dried food import
- Need **SFA registration to import processed food products** via GoBusiness. No annual fee, but a fee per consignment, a GIRO account with SFA, and a **TradeNet import permit per shipment**. Imports must come from approved sources and meet labeling rules. Some items (meat, seafood, eggs, fresh produce) have stricter requirements.
- Source: [SFA commercial food imports](https://sfa.gov.sg/food-import-export/commercial-food-imports), [SFA licensing and registration of traders](https://www.SFA.gov.sg/food-import-export/licensing-registration-of-traders).
- **Open question:** exact category for the intended products. Needs checking per product.

### Pet food import
- Pet food, treats or supplements **containing meat or meat products need AVS pre-import approval**. For countries outside Australia, NZ, Canada, UK and USA, the manufacturing plant needs a technical dossier approved (HACCP-type certificates, analysis, process flow). Commercial import, manufacture or sale of non-food-animal feed requires an NParks/AVS licence.
- Source: [AVS guidelines for importing pet food](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/30/b3ab79c4-5fdd-4c77-a49f-68e72d1e80ba/Guidelines%20for%20importing%20pet%20food%20(Non-scheduled%20Countries)%2021%20Apr%202026.pdf).
- **Takeaway:** significantly more paperwork than general dried food, unless you import non-meat products or source from a scheduled country.

### Digital and services
- No licence needed in general. Consider PDPA (data protection) compliance if you collect customer data.
