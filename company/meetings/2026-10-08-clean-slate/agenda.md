# Board meeting: clean-slate idea generation

*Date: 2026-10-08. Called by the Chairman (Caleb).*

## Question

What are the best business ideas for the Chairman to research deeply, starting from a clean slate? Output: a **ranked shortlist of 5 ideas** (plus 2 reserves) that survive attack from every robot.

## Constraints (from `01-constraints.md`, binding)

- Singapore market. Capital S$5k to S$20k. About 10 hrs/week. Profit within 6 to 12 months.
- Go-to-market via **partnerships** (resellers, referrers, channel partners). The Chairman has done direct outreach and vibecodes MVPs.
- Physical products are fine. Demand must be structural, not a fad.
- Seed ideas the Chairman mentioned: imported dried foods, pet food, website-builder / training app / consultancy for sports coaches.

## Old list

`02-ideas.md` holds a gut-level list of 25 ideas from the first pass. It is **unresearched**. Treat it as input to judge, not as truth. Keep what is good, cut what is bad, say why.

## Format (3 rounds, parallel within a round)

Files live in this folder. **Each robot writes only its own files** (parallel runs, so no shared file edits).

| Round | Who | Writes | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PROBE, BEACON, SERVO, LEDGER, COGSWORTH (parallel) | `r1-<robot>.md` | Propose 6 to 8 fresh ideas from their own lens, then judge the old list |
| 2 | Same five (parallel) | `r2-<robot>.md` | Read everyone's Round 1. Attack, merge, back or kill ideas. Name your top 5 |
| 3 | RIVET-CEO | `synthesis.md` | Ranked shortlist, dissent kept visible, questions for the Chairman |

## Idea card (required for every idea)

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name | Short |
| Buyer | A named role, not "people" |
| Offer + revenue model | What is sold, price range, one-off or recurring |
| Channel partners | At least 2 types in Singapore |
| Startup cost | Rough S$, labeled estimate or sourced |
| Weekly hours | After launch |
| Why demand lasts | Structural driver |
| Biggest risk | The one thing most likely to kill it |

## Rules

Per `company/PROTOCOL.md`. Also:
- About 500 words max per file. Tables over prose.
- Numbers need a source URL and date, or the label "estimate".
- Web search is allowed and encouraged for current Singapore facts. Do not contact anyone or spend money.
- Round 2: agree only as "I tried to break X and couldn't, because <evidence>".
