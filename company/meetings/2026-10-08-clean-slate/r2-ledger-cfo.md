**CNY 2027 deadline.** CNY is Sat 6 Feb 2027 ([source](https://patchandbagel.com/blogs/corporate-gifts-guides-tips/chinese-new-year-corporate-gifts-singapore-ordering-guide)). Order 10-12 weeks ahead: **14 Nov (12 wks) to 28 Nov (10 wks, latest)**. Deposits must be in by 14 Nov, so the go/no-go is needed by **22 Oct 2026**. Base needs about 72 hrs of outreach in 5 weeks, which is already above the 53 hrs the cap allows. Without a decision by 22 Oct, drop CNY and aim for Mid-Autumn 2027.

**Cap on B2B clients (hours-only ceiling).** Service products fill 10 hrs/wk at about 19-24 clients (CoachKit 45, PDPA 58). Steady profit is S$2.3-3.2k/mo base and S$1.2-1.6k downside (PDPA S$0.3-0.8k). The ceiling is hours, not demand. Two products cannot both fit under 10 hrs. S$2-3k/mo is a part-time wage, not "steady income", unless price per client rises or a partner does onboarding.

**Merges**
- Tuition desk, Lead responder and Helper-Cycle become one WhatsApp engine; the talk test picks the vertical.
- CoachKit absorbs SERVO G report add-on and COGSWORTH #3.
- Hamper, dried-food and gift-engine cards become one preorder-only CNY pilot, sealed packs only.

**Top 5**
1. WhatsApp engine, tuition/enrichment first: best base ceiling and margin, but only if the client pays the platform.
2. CoachKit at S$500 + S$49: lowest cash, but partner supply unproven.
3. CNY hamper pilot: S$3.7k base, only if the 22 Oct decision is made.
4. Lead responder: highest base profit, but SERVO's own churn risk.
5. Helper-Cycle: AEAS gives a named list of 330 agencies, but breakeven is slow (month 11 to S$1.5k).

**Kill:** PDPA-Pack standalone. Base profit S$0.6k at m12, S$0.04k downside, 25% referral leakage, plus legal liability. Keep only as a cosec add-on.

**Veto:** K6 used on any variant where ROD pays the WhatsApp platform (gross margin 12% / -5% / 17%, under the 60% digital floor). K7 used on the sub-S$80 hamper variant (about S$963 profit per season, less S$300-1,500 insurance, no profit path). K1 not used.
