## 6. The income-ceiling problem

At 10 hrs/week, B2B services cap at **S$1.7k/mo (COGS, 12-13 clients) to S$3.2k/mo (LEDGER, 19-24 clients)** in the base case, and S$1.2-1.6k in downside. Two products cannot both fit.

**Plain answer against `01-constraints.md`** ("profit in 6 to 12 months"): the base case meets it (S$1.5k/mo by month 4-6); the downside does not (S$0.3-0.4k at month 12). **Neither replaces a salary.** It is a part-time supplement.

| Option | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Raise ROD's fee, e.g. S$150 to S$250 | Cap about S$3k+ at 12-13 clients | All-in becomes about S$370 vs S$55 incumbents. Works only where no incumbent exists (enquiry desk, home services) |
| Raise hours | Roughly doubles the cap at 20 hrs (RIVET est.) | Only the Chairman can change `01-constraints.md` |
| Productize | Partners do onboarding, about 17 clients | Partners unproven. Multi-tenant software is vetoed |
| Stepping stone | S$1.5-3k/mo bridge while a physical line (#3, #4, #5) grows without hours | Stock risk, lumpy |

**Recommendation:** treat it as a stepping stone and price-first. Test both S$150 and S$250 ROD fees in the talk tests. Do not raise hours yet. Revisit at month 6 against actuals.
