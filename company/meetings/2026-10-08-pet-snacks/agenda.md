# Board meeting: premium pet snacks (focused brainstorm)

*Date: 2026-10-08. Called by the Chairman (Caleb). Approved: 10 runs, parallel within each round.*

## Why this meeting

The Chairman asked the board to brainstorm around **premium pet snacks** (dehydrated or freeze-dried treats; "check what licences are needed for meat"). Pet food was one of the Chairman's original seeds, but in earlier meetings it was only screened, never worked up. Baseline to beat: **Club and Coach Desk** (S$2.5k good case / S$0.3k bad case at month 12, about 7 hrs/week; see `company/meetings/2026-10-09-ideation-2/synthesis.md`).

## Question

Can premium pet snacks be a durable business at 20 hrs/week and S$5k to S$20k in Singapore? Which **version** of the idea works (product, species, meat or non-meat, import vs make vs private-label, channel), what licences does each version need, and does any version beat the baseline?

## Constraints (binding, from `01-constraints.md`)

- Singapore. Capital S$5k to S$20k. About 20 hrs/week. Partnership-led selling preferred. Steady income in 6 to 12 months.
- Stay away from the day-job industry (modular and prefabricated buildings, construction).
- Founder strengths: athletics network, entrepreneurial friends, vibecoding, website knowledge, business knowledge. Physical products are fine.
- Demand must be structural, not a fad.
- **Income ladder:** say whether each version can reach S$2k / S$4k / S$6k a month net, and when (label estimates).
- Already known (`04-setup-costs.md`, checked 2026-10-08): meat-containing pet food needs AVS pre-approval; SFA registration and TradeNet permits apply to food imports. **Verify and extend these with current sources.**
