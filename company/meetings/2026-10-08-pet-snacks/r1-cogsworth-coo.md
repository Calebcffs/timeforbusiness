# R1 COGSWORTH-COO: licensing and operations (vetoes off)

*Sources read 2026-10-08. AVS = NParks Animal & Veterinary Service. No fee or lead time below is mine unless marked "est."*

## 1. Correction to `04-setup-costs.md`
Pet treats are an **AVS** matter, not SFA. SFA's feed licence covers feed for food-producing animals [C]. I found no SFA step for imported finished pet treats. SFA only matters if we buy meat or fish ingredients ourselves for local production (est., unverified).

## 2. What each category needs (import)

| Category | Pre-import approval | Per-shipment | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Non-meat** (plant, veg, probably fish and insect) | None. Manufacturer's declaration "no meat", ingredient list | CCP via TradeNet, S$22, 1 working day | [A] [B] |
| **Meat, from AU / NZ / CA / UK / US** ("scheduled") | None | Health certificate from exporting vet authority + CCP S$22 | [A] [B] |
| **Meat, heat-treated, from EU / Japan** (BSE-negligible, FMD-free) | Online self-declaration before arrival | Health certificate + CCP | [D] |
| **Meat, raw / frozen / freeze-dried, or any other origin** (Thailand, China, Malaysia) | **PFA technical dossier** from the factory: composition, COA, label, process flow, HACCP, licences, sample health cert | Health cert + CCP | [D] |
| **Beef** from non-BSE-negligible origin | Not allowed | n/a | [D] |

- "Meat" is listed as beef, lamb, chicken, meat/bone meal. Whether fish or insect count as meat is **not stated** in the pages I read. A search summary said no, but that is an inference. Confirm with AVS before betting on it.
- Gelatin, collagen, glucosamine and chondroitin need an animal-or-synthetic declaration [B].
- Dossier lead time is **not published**. Est. 4 to 12 weeks, plus the factory's willingness to share documents.
- Registering as an importer on GoBusiness is "not mandatory" [B]. Needs a GIRO account with AVS and a TradeNet/Customs account. The Feeding Stuffs Act text says a licence is needed to import or sell feed [E]. That conflicts with [B]. **Ask AVS in writing.**
- The legal importer must be a registered entity. The PFA cover letter needs a company registration number and a recall contact [D]. **Use a Pte Ltd** (liability).
- Labels must be in English: manufacturer and importer names and addresses, ingredients, manufacture and expiry dates, directions, analysis [D].

## 3. What each category needs (local make)

| Item | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Licence | AVS licence to manufacture, S$240/yr, about 21 working days | [F] |
| Premises | **Not** HDB or private residential. Needs URA/HDB/JTC permission and NEA approval. SFA says food processing establishments cannot make or store pet food [C] | [F] [C] |
| Documents | ACRA profile, layout plan, process flow, SOPs, labels | [F] |
| Implication | A commercial unit and a lease. This hits the "no lease" rule in `01-constraints.md`. Est. S$1.5k to 3k/month for a small industrial unit | est. |
| Escape | **Private label** through an AVS-licensed maker. Pet Axis (Singapore) claims to be the only SG private-label pet food producer and does freeze-dried [G]. The maker holds the licence. Whether a brand owner needs its own is unconfirmed. MOQ and price unknown. | [G] |

## 5. Recurring operating load (est.)
Per shipment: TradeNet declaration and documents about 1.5 hrs; one or two shipments a month. Support, orders and packing: 3 to 6 hrs/wk at 100 orders a month. Storage: dry goods in a spare room are fine, freeze-dried is shelf-stable but sensitive to humidity. Shelf life and sealing (oxygen absorbers, barrier pouches) come from the maker.

## 6. Which three I would test first
1. **#6 (AU/NZ freeze-dried)** or **#7 (private-label freeze-dried)**: premium story, a legal path with no dossier (scheduled origin) or no licence for us (local maker), and the only versions that plausibly reach S$4k.
2. **#1 (curated bundles)**: zero licence risk and a fast demand probe in the athletics and dog-owner network, before any inventory.
3. **#3 or #4 (insect or fish)**: only if AVS confirms in writing that they are non-meat. That would make them the cheapest regulated import.

Chairman action (a form and an email, not a blocker): ask AVS whether fish, insect and brand-owner selling need a licence or registration.
