## Cash cycle and risks

| Item | Base case | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| Cash tied up (#1/#2) | Order 20 kg, sell through in 3-4 mo; payback ~month 4-6 | Products fail talk test; S$1.5-3k stock expires (FD shelf life 12-24 mo, estimate) |
| Cash tied up (#3/#4) | S$7k MOQ locked; payback ~month 9-12 | Stock at risk equals 35-50% of the S$20k budget; **worst cash cycle** |
| CAC (estimate) | S$5 via partners, S$15-30 paid social | DTC at S$25 CAC eats ~2.7 packs of margin; first-order payback fails on one-pack buyers |
| Per-shipment permits | Spread over 400+ packs, ~S$0.10-0.30/pack | Small repeated shipments erode margin |

## Which 3 to test first

1. **#2 non-meat FD fruit/veg import (or #6 as a zero-licence fallback).** Avoids AVS, small MOQ, S$3-5k at risk, reaches S$2k at ~290 packs/mo. Cheapest to prove price and repeat.
2. **#1 China FD chicken/fish**, but only after COGSWORTH confirms the AVS dossier cost; the 67% margin is meaningless if the dossier costs S$3k+. Highest margin on paper, highest paperwork.
3. **#10 canicross niche via athletics network.** Highest price and fit with the Chairman's network. Tests whether a narrow premium story beats generic treats.

Honest base case: S$2k/mo plausible by about month 9-12 for #1/#2; S$4k and S$6k need partner wholesale volume (#3/#5/#6) or 500-800 active subscribers, which I rate unlikely at 20 hrs/week within 12 months. Baseline (Club and Coach Desk) is S$2.5k good case at 7 hrs/week, so no pet version clearly beats it on hours.
