## 3. Regulation (extends 04-setup-costs.md)

| Item | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Meat-containing treats, non-scheduled countries | AVS pre-approval of product **and plant**. **Heat-treated** products from EU/Japan-type (BSE-negligible, FMD-free) countries need only an online self-declaration. **Raw, frozen and freeze-dried** need a full Pet Food Assessment dossier (composition, COA, HACCP/ISO certificates, process flow, health certificate). Permit S$22 per consignment, AVS GIRO account. | [AVS guideline 21 Apr 2026](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/30/b3ab79c4-5fdd-4c77-a49f-68e72d1e80ba/Guidelines%20for%20importing%20pet%20food%20(Non-scheduled%20Countries)%2021%20Apr%202026.pdf) (read in full) |
| Scheduled countries (AU, NZ, CA, UK, US) | Lighter route per search summary; **not read in full**, unknown | [AVS](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/-/media/avs/migrated-content/animals-and-pets/bringing-animals-into-singapore-and-exporting/animal-feed/guidelines-for-importing-pet-food-from-scheduled-countries.pdf) |
| Local manufacture | AVS Animal Feed Licence S$240/yr, ~21 working days, **no home production in HDB or private homes**, needs approved commercial premises (URA/HDB/JTC and NEA approval). | Labelling | English; ingredients, manufacture and expiry dates, species, importer name and address | AVS summary via search (not read in full) |

Implication: a home-kitchen dehydrator business is not licensable. Pawfect-style "home-based" operation looks non-compliant on the AVS wording (unverified whether they hold a licence).

## 4. Demand stability (K3 lens, no veto in R1)

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Need older than 5 years? | Pet ownership: yes (84k dogs in 2024 vs 70k in 2019). Premium freeze-dried treats specifically: **unknown**, no time series found. |
| Growth | Total category: slow (+3.2% CAGR, Statista). Cat segment: step-change from licensing and the HDB cap, but 2 cats per HDB flat caps growth per household. |
| Structural driver? | Yes for ownership and humanisation. **Not proven** for freeze-dried format; may be a trend inside a structural market. Euromonitor also flags "price polarisation", which means a squeeze in the middle. |
