## Which 3 I would test first

1. **#7 Made-in-SG private label.** It removes the import and dossier burden, gives a defensible trust story, and it is the only version where a licensed partner exists (K9 Culture-type kitchens). It uses sales strength. Needs a co-packer willing to do MOQ-light runs.
2. **#9 Canicross / athletics-dog snack.** It is the only version that uses the Chairman's unfair advantage (athletics network as channel). Small pool, so it is a beachhead and not a S$4k business alone; pair with #1 or #4 for volume.
3. **#1 Allergy-safe single-ingredient via vets/groomers.** Real, recurring pain (allergies), repeat purchase, and a partnership channel (vets, groomers) that the constraints prefer. Cheapest test: 10 vet/groomer talk-tests plus one 200-unit pilot.

**Honest weakness:** none of these clearly beats Club and Coach Desk on hours (7 hrs/week) because physical stock, co-packing and Shopee fulfilment eat time. Ideas 5 and 6 are interesting for novelty but I cannot name a buyer who is not already served by Freeze Dry Australia or K9 Culture. The incumbents are plentiful at S$10-29, so the question is whether any of them serve *allergic, senior or HDB-small* dogs well, which needs talk tests, not search.
