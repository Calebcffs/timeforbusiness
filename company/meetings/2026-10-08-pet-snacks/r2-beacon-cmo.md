# R2 BEACON-CMO: path to customers, vetoes ON (K5)

*2026-10-08. All conversion and sell-through figures are **estimates** unless a source is named. I cut my own R1 ranges roughly in half because my blind spot is overestimating conversion and partner enthusiasm.*

## 1. Bottom line

**Is there a differentiated angle? Not one I can evidence.** PROBE found at least 4 local freeze-dried brands: Singapaw, Holuah, Taki Pets and K-Nine Culture. Their prices sit at S$10-16 per 40-50 g pack and they sell through Polypet, Watsons, FairPrice and Lazada (PROBE r1, 2026-10-08). A new freeze-dried single-protein SKU is the fifth entrant with no reason for a shop to drop an existing brand. The "premium" ceiling is about S$27/100 g (PROBE), so there is no price-led wedge either.

The only wedges named in R1 are hypotheses with no demand evidence:

| Wedge | Evidence it is real pain | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Allergy or senior dog (SPARK #1, #3) | None. SPARK says Singapore complaint data was "thin" | Untested |
| Toy-breed mini bits for HDB-approved breeds (SPARK #4) | None. Sizes of 15-40 g already exist (Kit Cat 15 g, Singapaw minis 40 g) | Untested, partly served |
| Canicross or running-dog recovery (SPARK #9, LEDGER #10, my R1 #5) | I found no Singapore canicross group | Unknown pool size |
| "Recall-proof" traceable treats (SPARK #10) | One recall in 5 years (Sportmix, 2021) | Weak; S$12-20k at risk |

## 3. First 10 customers, honest version

| Step | Action | Hours | Conversion (estimate, halved) | Result |

| 1 | Walk into 60 groomers, cafes, independent shops with samples (CRM method) | 25 | 25-40% take a sample | 15-24 |

So 10 *trial* accounts is reachable but takes about 120 visits (est.). That is roughly 50 hours before any real revenue. Ten *reordering* accounts is not credible inside the first quarter. Each account is worth about S$70-100 contribution a month (est.), so 10 accounts is about S$0.7-1k. The Chairman has no named shop, groomer or vet contact (their own open question in `01-constraints.md`).
