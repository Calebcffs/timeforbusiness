## 1. Open r1 points: what is now settled

| Question | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Fish / insect = "meat"? | [N] and [S] list "beef, lamb, chicken, meat/bone meal, **etc**". Fish and insect are not named, and "etc" means the list is open. No AVS page I could read defines meat. | **Unverified.** It only matters for non-scheduled, heat-treated fish or insect (e.g. a Thai fish-skin treat). Freeze-dried from non-scheduled origins needs a dossier either way. Scheduled origins need only a health cert. |
| Brand owner needs own registration? | [N] cover letter lists "importer" and "exporter **or brand owner**" separately. No registration is asked of the brand owner. AVS import registration is "not mandatory" [P]. The importer needs a company number, a GIRO account and TradeNet. | **Likely no for an importer-of-record Pte Ltd. Unverified for a brand that only sells.** |
| Licence to import or sell feed? | [FSA] (search summary of the 2000 text): no import, manufacture or sale of feed without a licence, S$240/yr. This conflicts with [P]. | **Unverified.** Worst case S$240/yr and about 21 working days (AVS licence page, r1 [F]). That is a form and a fee, **not K4**. |
| Scheduled route (r1 gap) | [S]: AU/NZ/CA/UK/US need **no pre-approval**. Each shipment needs a vet health certificate, CCP S$22, 1 working day [P]. Cert item (j): country FMD-free for 6 months **or** process certified to inactivate FMD. AU and NZ are FMD-free, so (j)(a) should apply. Freeze Dry Australia is on Watsons shelves (PROBE), which suggests the route works. UK beef is out (UK not BSE-negligible, [S] footnote). | Read in full. |

**New finding:** both guidelines require the label to show importer name and address and a "guaranteed analysis in a complete and balanced diet" (NRC/AAFCO) [S][N]. Treats are not complete diets. Imported stock may need relabelling.
