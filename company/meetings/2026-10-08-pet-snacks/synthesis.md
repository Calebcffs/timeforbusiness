## 3. Licensing in plain English: AVS, not SFA

| Route | Needs |
|---|---|
| Non-meat | Maker's "no meat" declaration; S$22 permit per shipment (TradeNet, 1 day) |
| Meat from AU/NZ/CA/UK/US ("scheduled") | No pre-approval; vet health certificate plus permit (UK beef excluded) |
| Meat, heat-treated, EU/Japan | Online self-declaration, certificate, permit |
| Raw/frozen/freeze-dried meat, other origins | Factory dossier, 4-12 weeks (est.); K4 doubt for China/Thailand |
| Make it ourselves | AVS licence S$240/yr, about 21 working days, commercial premises only (no home kitchens; breaks "no lease") |
| Private label | Licensed maker holds the licence; Pet Axis MOQ/price unknown |

Always: English label with importer name and address, a company (Pte Ltd advised for liability; the dossier route requires a company number), AVS GIRO account.

**Unverified; Chairman sends via www.avs.gov.sg/feedback (personal email, not work Outlook):**

4. Does the "complete and balanced diet" label clause apply to treats?
