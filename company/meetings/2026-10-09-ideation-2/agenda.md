# Board meeting: ideation round 2 (wide)

*Date: 2026-10-09. Called by the Chairman (Caleb).*

## Why this meeting

The first shortlist (meeting of 2026-10-08) felt "so-so". Its ideas were narrow because robots ideated through their job lens, had no data on the Chairman's strengths, applied vetoes while ideas were forming, and worked under a 10 hrs/week cap. Time is now **20 hrs/week**. The Chairman's favourite from round 1 was **Club and Coach Desk**, by a small margin. It is the **baseline**: new ideas should beat it.

## Question

Produce a wide, original pool of business ideas, cluster it, then attack and rank the best ones. Output: a ranked shortlist of **5 ideas plus 2 reserves** that are better than Club and Coach Desk, or a clear statement that none is.

## Constraints (from `01-constraints.md`, binding)

- Singapore. Capital S$5k to S$20k. **About 20 hrs/week.** Partnership-led selling preferred.
- **Stay away from the day-job industry** (modular and prefabricated buildings, construction) and employer resources.
- Founder strengths: athletics network, entrepreneurial friends, vibecoding, website knowledge, business knowledge.
- Demand must be structural, not a fad. Physical products are fine.
- **Income ladder:** for each idea, say whether it can plausibly reach S$2k, S$4k or S$6k a month net at 20 hrs/week, and about how long that takes (label estimates).

## Round 2 task (SPARK-CIO only)

Read all six Round 1 files. Cluster the ideas into named groups, merge true duplicates, flag which ideas beat Club and Coach Desk on the income ladder, and rank the **top 10 clusters by interest**, not by safety. Do not kill anything.

## Round 3 task (core robots, vetoes on)

Attack the top 10 clusters from your own lens. For each: assumption, severity (Fatal/Major/Minor), cheapest test. State which ideas you would veto and why, and give your own top 5. Compare each against Club and Coach Desk.
