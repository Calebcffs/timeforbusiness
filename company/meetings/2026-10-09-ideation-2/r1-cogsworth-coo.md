## Top 3 to test

**#5 (first-aid refill subscription)** is the only row where the law creates the demand and the revenue recurs, so it tests the "recurring physical B2B" model; the risk is that each site is small, so the talk test is how many sites one person can service in the hours. **#9 (studio consumables subscription)** is the one row that plausibly reaches S$4k on repeat orders alone, and the Chairman's entrepreneurial friends who run gyms and studios make a fast 5-conversation talk test. **#2 (medals and awards)** is the cheapest way to start selling into the athletics network; it needs no licence, takes a deposit before ordering, and a first meet gives a real paid pilot within weeks. I would test #4 next, since GeBIZ is free to enter and school buying is annual.
