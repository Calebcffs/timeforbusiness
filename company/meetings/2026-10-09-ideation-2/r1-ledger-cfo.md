## Top 3 I would test first

**#1 Team kits, #2 merch, #5 concierge.** Kits and merch are the only ideas here where the customer's deposit funds the stock, so cash risk is near zero and gross margin is about 60%, versus 39% on coffee and 20-30% on the shoe fee. Both reorder annually. Kits also sit on the Chairman's athletics network, which gives a 5-call talk test this week. Concierge is the cheapest test of all (zero stock, full prepayment) and tells us if the athletics network will pay for imports, which decides ideas 4, 5 and 11. Honest ceiling: kits and merch reach S$2k in months 4-9 on 85-320 units a month, but S$4k needs repeat B2B accounts, so they are a bridge, not a salary. Ideas 11 and 12 are the only ones that plausibly touch S$6k, and only by spending nearly all the capital.
