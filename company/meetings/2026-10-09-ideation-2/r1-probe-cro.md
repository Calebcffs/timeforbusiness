**Top 3 to test, and why.** (1) **#5 Healthier SG clinics.** It is the only idea here tied to both a state programme with money behind it and the largest demographic shift, and it routes through GP clinics as a partner. The clinic's willingness to refer is the real unknown, and 5 talk-test calls settle it. (2) **#1 Coach tracker.** The deadlines have passed, so what is left is recurring renewal and new-coach churn. It is the closest to Club and Coach Desk, so a talk test with 5 club admins tells us whether compliance is a standalone pain or only a feature. (3) **#3 Heat-stress kits.** It has the hardest deadline (1 Dec 2026) and sells to businesses outside the founder's day job, so it can be tested in two weeks with 5 calls to cleaning or landscaping firms. If the deadline passes untested, the one-time window is lost.
