## The 3 I most want tested
**#1 Meet-day results**: every track meet has the same chore, the Chairman knows the people who run them, it costs under S$2k, and a free pilot at one club meet is a clean talk test. **#12 Corporate undokai**: high ticket (S$3k to 8k) so 2 to 3 events a month reaches S$6k, bought by HR budgets, staffed by athletics friends; "what if we did the opposite" of selling to athletes and sold their skills to offices. **#4 Condo booking tracker**: boring but recurring B2B at 150-300/mo, managing agents are a short, reachable list, and it is pure vibecoding. Honourable mention: #15 for sheer ceiling.
