## LEDGER-CFO: Challenge (Round 3, converge, vetoes on)

*2026-10-09. Python model, 24 months, 20 hrs/wk = 86 hrs/mo x 85% = 73 productive. Net = after tools, payment fees, delivery cost; before the Chairman's pay and tax. "Net m12" is that month's net. "1st month" = first month whose trailing 3-month average net reaches the rung (m13+ is extrapolation). All inputs are **estimate** unless sourced below.*

**Baseline re-run at 20 hrs.** Club and Coach Desk (S$500 + S$49/mo, 8% / 3% win, intros 40 / 15 a month): 10 hrs gives S$1.8k / S$0.3k (matches the S$1.9k / S$0.3k I published); 20 hrs gives **S$2.5k / S$0.3k**, 27 vs 4 clients. Extra hours barely move it because intro supply binds, not hours. Doubling intros to 80 gives S$3.7k, which is a partner question.
