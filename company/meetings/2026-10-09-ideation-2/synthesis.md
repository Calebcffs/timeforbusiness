## 1. Verdict

**Nothing clearly beats Club and Coach Desk (CCD): a tie on evidence, a split on paper.**

- **Conflict:** they need 14-18 hrs/wk against CCD's 7. But LEDGER found CCD's limit is partner intros, not hours (20 hrs gives S$2.5k, 10 hrs S$1.8k). The challengers face the same limit from a smaller pool: 10 friends give 3-6 sites a round (BEACON); corporate needs about 76 warm HR talks a year (LEDGER).
- The comparison is unfair as run: CCD plus kits and awards fits in about 15 hrs (COGS) and **nobody modelled it**. That is #3 and a LEDGER action.
